# Wiggins Hair --- Premium Wig E‑Commerce Website

A modern, elegant, and conversion-focused e-commerce website concept
created for a premium wig and hair brand. The project is designed to
make discovering, comparing, and shopping for wigs feel simple,
polished, and visually engaging.

The design uses a clean white layout with warm brown and gold accents to
complement the Wiggins Hair branding. Product-focused imagery,
promotional banners, clear navigation, collection discovery, product
recommendations, and strong calls to action work together to create a
premium shopping experience across the entire website.

## Project Overview

This website concept includes the complete customer journey --- from
discovering featured products on the homepage to browsing collections,
exploring the full catalog, viewing detailed product information,
learning about the brand, and getting in touch.

### Main Pages

-   **Home** --- Promotional hero banners, featured categories, best
    sellers, new arrivals, seasonal promotions, trending products,
    occasion-based shopping, and recommended products.
-   **Shop All** --- Full product catalog with collection filters,
    sorting, product grid, pagination, recently viewed products, and
    promotional content.
-   **Collection** --- Visual collection directory designed to help
    shoppers quickly discover products by category, trend, occasion,
    recommendation, and new arrivals.
-   **Product Details** --- Large product gallery, pricing, sale
    information, product details, variants, quantity controls, Add to
    Cart / Buy Now actions, recommendations, and customer reviews.
-   **About** --- Brand-focused storytelling that communicates quality,
    confidence, comfort, natural beauty, and the overall value of the
    wig collection.
-   **Contact** --- Clean contact form for customer questions, support,
    collaborations, and general inquiries.

## Design Direction

The interface follows a minimal luxury aesthetic with generous spacing,
clear typography, warm brand colors, and product-first layouts. Reusable
navigation and footer sections help maintain consistency across pages,
while promotional banners and lifestyle imagery give the store a
stronger editorial feel.

The overall goal is to create an online wig-shopping experience that
feels **premium, trustworthy, easy to navigate, and focused on
conversion**.
